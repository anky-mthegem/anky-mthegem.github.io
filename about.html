About Me

Associate Manager | Mechatronics Engineer | Digital Manufacturing (BITS Pilani)

Professional Profile & Summary

I am a Mechatronics Engineer with 9+ years of comprehensive hands-on experience in industrial robotics, robotic cell automation, PLC programming (Siemens TIA Portal, Beckhoff, TwinCAT), computer vision integration, and high-throughput manufacturing plant commissioning.

Currently serving as Associate Manager at Godrej & Boyce Mfg. Co. Ltd (Interio Division), I manage the project team driving Infrastructure development, plant capacity & capability enhancement, Automation, Robotics, and Plant Commissioning.

"Proven track record in reducing manufacturing cycle times, integrating advanced vision & safety modules, and leveraging Industry 4.0 & Digital Manufacturing to maximize operational efficiency."

My career spans successful implementations across diverse industrial environments:

  • Godrej & Boyce: IoT implementation for Stitching Cell at Haridwar Plant (60% process improvement), Cupboard assembly line commissioning (Khalapur Plant), Automatic shutter bending & corner forming (Khalapur Plant), Robotic chemical foam pouring & releasing agent setup with KUKA conveyor tracking (Haridwar Plant), SVG power quality system (Haridwar Plant), Vision-guided dynamic robot movement programming, and in-house YOLO vision inspection integrated with Siemens S7-1200 PLC for automatic FG warehouse transfer.
  • Bharat Milk Products: Complete automated Clean-In-Place (CIP) plant installation and commissioning using Siemens S7-1200 PLC, CTQ calibration, and ERP deployment.
  • DCM Engineering: Loramendi automatic core-making machines, ABB foundry robotics programming for Mahindra S101 block & Eicher lines, achieving a 30% cycle time reduction and 66% shift output increase.

Currently pursuing M.Tech in Digital Manufacturing from Birla Institute of Technology and Science (BITS Pilani), and holding a B.Tech in Mechatronics from Lovely Professional University (2017), I bridge advanced theoretical engineering with execution on the shop floor.
