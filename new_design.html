Engineering Blueprint

Motorcycle Architecture & Telemetry

Technical breakdown of the Harley-Davidson X440 S through the analytical lens of a mechatronics engineer.

Displacement & Cycle Single Cyl
440 cc Long-Stroke

Single-cylinder, 2-valve, air-and-oil-cooled engine with dedicated auxiliary oil cooler radiator for thermal endurance on long highway tours.

Peak Torque Output Dyno Proven
38 Nm @ 4,000 RPM

Flat, muscular torque curve with over 90% torque available from 2,000 RPM, delivering effortless passing speed and rich exhaust cadence.

Maximum Power Crank Output
27 BHP @ 6,000 RPM

Linear power curve mapped specifically for unstrained cruising at 100–120 km/h with low engine stress and minimal vibration.

Drivetrain & Clutch Transmission
6-Speed Assist & Slipper

Feather-light clutch effort reduces city fatigue; integrated slipper mechanism prevents rear-wheel hopping during aggressive downshifts.

Front Suspension KYB Cartridge
43mm USD Inverted Forks

Substantial 43mm upside-down cartridge forks minimize unsprung weight, providing rigid chassis tracking and precise front-end cornering bite.

Rear Suspension Twin Shock
Gas-Charged 7-Step Preload

Twin gas-pressurized shock absorbers with 7 preload adjustments accommodating solo riding or fully loaded touring panniers.

Braking Hardware ByBre Brembo
320mm Front / 240mm Rear

Large 320mm front rotor actuated by ByBre calipers and managed by dual-channel ABS for immediate, predictable deceleration.

Frame Architecture Backbone
Steel Trellis Frame

High-tensile tubular steel chassis with the engine serving as a stressed structural member for low center of gravity and high torsional rigidity.

Instrumentation Digital Display
3.5" Circular Color TFT

High-contrast circular TFT with ambient light sensing, real-time speed, tachometer needle animation, gear indicator, and trip telemetry.

Connected Telematics Bluetooth BLE
Turn-by-Turn Navigation

Displays real-time navigation directions, incoming caller ID, music playback status, and phone battery percentage directly on screen.

Lighting System Signature LED
Projector Headlamp & DRL Ring

Signature horizontal chrome-trimmed bar with dual LED projectors and full-perimeter daytime running halo for exceptional night vision.

Safety Telemetry Electronic
Dual-Channel ABS System

Continuous wheel-speed sensors feeding real-time slip calculations to modulate brake line pressure and prevent wheel locking.

Fuel Range Touring Tank
13.5 Liters (~450 km Range)

Classic sculpted teardrop steel tank with internal fuel anti-surge baffles delivering 400 to 450 km of highway range between pitstops.

Saddle Ergonomics Rider Comfort
805 mm Seat Height

Contoured touring saddle with mid-set foot controls and wide flat-tracker handlebars ensuring upright posture and minimal back strain.

Underside Clearance Terrain Stance
170 mm Ground Clearance

Generous clearance allowing confident traversal over rough bypass detours, speed humps, and unpaved mountain roads.

Wheelbase & Geometry Highway Composure
1,418 mm Wheelbase

Generous straight-line wheelbase ensuring reassuring crosswind stability at expressway speeds and steady tracking through highway sweepers.
